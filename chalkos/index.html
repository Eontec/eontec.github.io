The Hardware Crisis

Underfunded schools cannot afford to buy new computers every three years.

A modern computer science education is essential, but the hardware barrier is insurmountable for millions of students. Relying on heavy, bloated operating systems forces schools into an expensive cycle of forced obsolescence.

"We're throwing away laptops that are mechanically fine, simply because modern software demands more RAM than schools can afford."

Built to Take a Beating

Students learn by exploring, and exploring means breaking things.

Traditional systems lock students out of administrative rights entirely, stifling curiosity. ChalkOS uses a unique "Sandwich Architecture" that gives them full control over an isolated Sandbox layer.

The 10-Second Recovery

When a student inevitably deletes a critical system file or accidentally bricks the OS, recovery is instantaneous. A simple reboot wipes the active Sandbox layer, reverting the laptop to a pristine state in under ten seconds. Zero IT tickets required. Zero lost learning time.
